## Getting there
Take **Amtrak from DC Union Station to Moynihan Train Hall**. It's about 3 hours and drops you in Midtown, so it beats flying once you count airport time. Northeast Regional is the cheaper train and Acela is the faster one.

## Where to stay
Midtown West, near Penn Station or Bryant Park. You walk straight from the train, and most subway lines run through there.

## Day 1 (Wed): Arrive and see the West Side
- Take a morning train, drop your bags and grab lunch.
- Walk **Hudson Yards → the High Line ![High Line](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5a/High_Line_Park%2C_Section_1a.jpg/330px-High_Line_Park%2C_Section_1a.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) → Chelsea Market → Little Island**.
- Evening: Times Square, then a Broadway show.

## Day 2 (Thu): Lower Manhattan and Brooklyn
- Catch the **first ferry from Battery Park** to the Statue of Liberty ![Statue of Liberty](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg/330px-Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Ellis Island. Book through Statue City Cruises, the only official seller. Pedestal and crown tickets sell out weeks ahead.
- Afternoon: 9/11 Memorial & Museum, the Oculus and Wall Street. Add the Museum of Jewish Heritage if you have time, since it's right there.
- Around sunset, walk the **Brooklyn Bridge** ![Brooklyn Bridge](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f0/Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg/330px-Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) into DUMBO.
- Dinner at **The River Café**, which sits under the bridge in DUMBO (it isn't near the Statue). For something cheaper, get pizza at Juliana's.

## Day 3 (Fri): Museums and Central Park
- Morning: **the Met** ![Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/30/Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_%28The_Met%29_-_Central_Park%2C_NYC.jpg/330px-Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_%28The_Met%29_-_Central_Park%2C_NYC.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail). Pick a few wings, because you can't see it all.
- Afternoon: walk Central Park past Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge and the Mall, then out to Columbus Circle.
- Sunset: **Top of the Rock** at Rockefeller Center.
- Splurge dinner: **Le Bernardin**. Reservations open about 30 days ahead.

## Day 4 (Sat): The Bronx and Harlem
- Morning: **New York Botanical Garden**, which is at its best in May, or the Bronx Zoo next door. If the Yankees are home, swap in a game or a stadium tour. The 2028 schedule should come out around late summer 2027.
- Lunch on **Arthur Avenue**, the Bronx's Little Italy.
- Afternoon: Harlem, including the Apollo Theater and 125th Street. Have soul food at Sylvia's or Red Rooster.

## Day 5 (Sun): Queens, then head home
- **Flushing Meadows**: see the Unisphere ![Unisphere](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg/330px-Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and the Queens Museum's Panorama of the City of New York.
- Lunch: dumplings in Flushing.
- Take the LIRR to Penn Station (about 20 minutes) and catch an afternoon train home.

**Staten Island** isn't on any of these days, but it's easy to add. The **Staten Island Ferry** is free and passes the Statue. Ride it on Day 2 if the Liberty Island tickets sell out.

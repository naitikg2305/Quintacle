# NYC, 5 days in 2028: a new plan

You already have this trip in your notes, but as two identical copies: [2028 NYC 5-Day Trip Itinerary](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned/new-york-2028&open=2028-nyc-5-day-trip-itinerary-local-local) and [2028 NYC Five-Borough Itinerary](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned/new-york-2028&open=2028-nyc-five-borough-itinerary-local-claude). - **Wrong boroughs for the hotels.** The Bowery Hotel and Hotel Gansevoort are in Manhattan, not Brooklyn and the Bronx. The William Vale is in Brooklyn, not Queens.
- **Wrong boroughs for some sights.** Coney Island is in Brooklyn, not Queens. Tompkins Square Park is in Manhattan, not Staten Island.

---

## Getting there
- **Train:** Amtrak Acela takes about 3h from DC Union Station to Moynihan/Penn Station. Northeast Regional takes about 3.5h and is usually much cheaper.
- **Getting around:** Use OMNY on the subway (about $3 a ride). A weekly cap means rides after the first 12 in 7 days are free. The Staten Island Ferry is free.

## Where to stay (pick one)
- **Midtown** (near Bryant Park or Grand Central) is easiest for subways to every borough.
- **Lower Manhattan / FiDi** is a bit cheaper, with quick access to the ferries and Brooklyn.

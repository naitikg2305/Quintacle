# 5 days in New York City, April 15–19, 2028

You already have this trip in your notes as two nearly identical copies: [5-Day New York City 2028 Trip Plan (local+claude)](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned&open=5-day-new-york-city-2028-trip-plan-local-claude) and [(local+local)](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned&open=5-day-new-york-city-2028-trip-plan-local-local). I kept their dates (Saturday April 15 to Wednesday April 19, 2028), the trip from DC, the Central Park hotel area and the ~$3,500–5,000 budget. I also fixed a few things in them that wouldn't work well on the ground.

## What I changed from your notes
- **One hotel for the whole trip.** The notes move you to Brooklyn on Day 2 and back again. Every borough is a subway or ferry ride from Midtown, so packing up costs you half a day for nothing.
- **No overnight train.** The Acela from DC's Union Station to New York Penn takes about 3 hours.
- **OMNY instead of MetroCard.** The MetroCard is being retired. Tap a contactless card or your phone at the turnstile.
- **Day 1 was too full.** The Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island take about 5 hours on their own. I moved them to a morning with nothing else around them.
- **"Staten Island Green"** isn't a place I recognize. I swapped in Snug Harbor.
- **Museum closing days.** The Met is closed on Wednesdays, and Le Bernardin is closed on Sundays. The plan below works around both.
- **April 16, 2028 is Easter Sunday.** That's a bonus: the Easter Parade happens on Fifth Avenue that day.

## Day 1 (Sat, Apr 15): Arrival and Midtown
- Morning Acela from DC, check in near Central Park South
- Central Park: Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge, the Mall. Mid-April is usually cherry blossom season.
- Empire State Building or Top of the Rock at sunset
- Late dinner at Katz's Delicatessen. It stays open late on weekends.

## Day 2 (Sun, Apr 16): Lower Manhattan and Staten Island
- Take the first ferry (~8:30) from Battery Park to the Statue of Liberty ![Statue of Liberty](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg/330px-Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Ellis Island
- Afternoon: ride the free Staten Island Ferry over, then visit Snug Harbor Cultural Center & Botanical Garden
- Evening: 9/11 Memorial, dinner in the Financial District
- Optional morning: the Easter Parade on Fifth Avenue, if you'd rather do that than the early ferry

## Day 3 (Mon, Apr 17): Brooklyn and Queens
- Walk the Brooklyn Bridge ![Brooklyn Bridge](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f0/Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg/330px-Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) in the morning, then DUMBO and Brooklyn Bridge Park
- Take the subway to Flushing Meadows: the Unisphere ![Unisphere](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg/330px-Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and the Queens Museum's Panorama of NYC (a huge scale model of the city)
- Dinner in Flushing (Chinese food) or Jackson Heights (South Asian and Latin)
- About Coney Island: it's a long ride from everything else, and in mid-April the rides are usually only open on weekends. I'd skip it unless you really want to see it.

## Day 4 (Tue, Apr 18): The Bronx and the Met
- Bronx Zoo when it opens at 10, lunch on Arthur Avenue (the Bronx's Little Italy)
- If the Yankees are home that night, you could see a game instead of the next two items. Their 2028 schedule should come out around August 2027.
- Afternoon at the Met (open until 5 on Tuesdays)
- Dinner at Le Bernardin (Michelin-starred)

## Day 5 (Wed, Apr 19): MoMA and heading home
- MoMA when it opens at 10:30
- Grand Central Terminal ![Grand Central Terminal](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/71/Image-Grand_central_Station_Outside_Night_2.jpg/330px-Image-Grand_central_Station_Outside_Night_2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Fifth Avenue
- Afternoon Acela back to DC

## Book ahead
- **Statue of Liberty:** buy from Statue City Cruises, the official seller. Crown tickets sell out months ahead.
- **Le Bernardin:** check when reservations open, probably about a month out.
- **MoMA and the Empire State Building or Top of the Rock:** buy timed tickets.
- **Acela:** fares go up closer to the date.

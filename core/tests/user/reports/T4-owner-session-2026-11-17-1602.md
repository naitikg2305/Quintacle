## Notes
- **Iron:** you mentioned earlier today that you'd realized iron matters (from a past chat, not your notes). 18 mg looks like a lot, but most of it comes from plants, which your body absorbs less well than iron from meat. The vitamin C at each main meal helps. Keep coffee and tea at least an hour away from breakfast and lunch. The dairy is already in separate meals, so its calcium won't compete with the iron.

A better place for the lamb is lunch: swap the chicken for **200 g of trimmed lean lamb leg** (about 280 kcal, 40 g protein). It's also the easiest way to get more absorbable iron, plus zinc and B12.
- **Weak spots:** for vitamin E, add 10 g of almonds (about 60 kcal). For vitamin D, a D3 supplement is worth considering on days without salmon, especially in a DC winter. Salmon every day is fine, but you could rotate it with sardines or trout.

I'm not a dietitian. If iron is a real concern, a blood test (ferritin) will tell you more than these estimates.

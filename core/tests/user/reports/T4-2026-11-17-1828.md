## Iron
You mentioned iron today. This plan gives about 3× the target.
- **Heme iron:** comes from the beef at lunch and is absorbed well.
- **Plant iron:** comes from the lentils and spinach. Every iron-rich meal includes vitamin C (orange, bell pepper, broccoli) to help you absorb it.
- **Coffee and tea:** have them at least an hour away from breakfast and lunch, because they reduce iron absorption.
- **Supplements:** don't take extra iron unless a blood test shows you're low. Too much iron is a real risk for men.

## Things that could leave gaps
- **Vitamin D:** depends on the salmon. Farmed Atlantic is fairly consistent. Without the salmon, you'd fall short on vitamin D.
- **Iodine:** use **iodized salt** when you cook. Dairy and eggs give some, but how much varies.
- **Vitamin K:** this plan has a lot. That only matters if you take a blood thinner.
- **Sodium:** most of it comes from the cottage cheese, so salt the other meals lightly.

## Swaps that keep the totals about the same
- **Beef:** 220 g chicken breast. You'd get much less iron and zinc, so I'd keep beef at least 3–4 times a week.
- **Salmon:** sardines or mackerel.
- **Lentils:** black beans or chickpeas, about the same dry weight.

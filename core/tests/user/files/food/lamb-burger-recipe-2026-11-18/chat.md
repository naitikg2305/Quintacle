## Lamb smash burgers (makes 2)

**Ingredients**
- 230 g Costco Lean Lamb, rolled into 2 balls of 115 g each. Keep them cold until they go in the pan.
- 2 eggs
- 2 Costco buns
- 70 g Atlantic salmon
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder, and maybe some cumin or chili flakes, which go well with lamb
- Optional: lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion

**Steps**
1. **Toast the buns** cut-side down in a dry pan. Set them aside.
2. **Heat a cast-iron pan** until it's very hot. Use a few drops of oil or none at all.
3. **Smash the patties.** Put a ball in the pan and press it hard and thin with a spatula, using parchment between them so it doesn't stick. Season the top right after smashing. Cook 2–2½ min until the edges get crispy, scrape it up, flip, and give it about 1 more minute.
4. **Fry the eggs** in the lamb fat left in the pan, one per burger. A runny yolk works as a sauce.
5. **Salmon**, pick one:
   - **On the side:** sear it skin-side down for 3–4 min, flip, give it another minute, and add lemon.
   - **On top:** flake the cooked salmon, mix it with a spoon of your Kirkland Greek yogurt, lemon and dill, and spread it on the bun like a sauce.
6. **Build each burger:** bun, patty, egg, salmon sauce if you're using it, veg, then the top bun.

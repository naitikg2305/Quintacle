# NYC, 5 days in 2028: a new plan

You already have this trip in your notes, but as two identical copies: [2028 NYC 5-Day Trip Itinerary](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned/new-york-2028&open=2028-nyc-5-day-trip-itinerary-local-local) and [2028 NYC Five-Borough Itinerary](/kb/browse?path=fun/travel/planned/new-york-2028&open=2028-nyc-five-borough-itinerary-local-claude). That draft has real errors, so I rebuilt the plan instead of reusing it:
- **Wrong boroughs for the hotels.** The Bowery Hotel and Hotel Gansevoort are in Manhattan, not Brooklyn and the Bronx. The William Vale is in Brooklyn, not Queens.
- **Wrong boroughs for some sights.** Coney Island is in Brooklyn, not Queens. Tompkins Square Park is in Manhattan, not Staten Island.
- **The River Café** isn't Italian, and it costs far more than $45 a person.
- **MetroCard is gone.** The subway now uses OMNY, where you tap a card or phone.
- **The Thanksgiving Parade** is in November, so it doesn't belong in a May trip.
- **The days don't line up.** Day 4 has you leaving for DC, and then Day 5 has you "departing from DC."

I kept your dates: **Mon May 15 – Fri May 19, 2028**, leaving from DC, with **one hotel the whole time** instead of switching every night.

---

## Getting there
- **Train:** Amtrak Acela takes about 3h from DC Union Station to Moynihan/Penn Station. Northeast Regional takes about 3.5h and is usually much cheaper.
- **Getting around:** Use OMNY on the subway (about $3 a ride). A weekly cap means rides after the first 12 in 7 days are free. The Staten Island Ferry is free.

## Where to stay (pick one)
- **Midtown** (near Bryant Park or Grand Central) is easiest for subways to every borough.
- **Lower Manhattan / FiDi** is a bit cheaper, with quick access to the ferries and Brooklyn.

## Day by day

**Day 1 (Mon): Arrive, Midtown**
- Morning train, check in by early afternoon.
- Visit Grand Central Terminal ![Grand Central Terminal](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/71/Image-Grand_central_Station_Outside_Night_2.jpg/330px-Image-Grand_central_Station_Outside_Night_2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Bryant Park, then walk Fifth Ave to Rockefeller Center.
- Watch the sunset from Top of the Rock ![Top of the Rock](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/db/GE_Building_by_David_Shankbone.JPG/330px-GE_Building_by_David_Shankbone.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail).
- Most Broadway shows are dark on Mondays, so save the show for Day 2.

**Day 2 (Tue): Lower Manhattan + Staten Island**
- Early ferry from Battery Park to the Statue of Liberty ![Statue of Liberty](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg/330px-Front_view_of_Statue_of_Liberty_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Ellis Island (half a day).
- 9/11 Memorial ![National September 11 Memorial & Museum](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fc/9-11_Memorial_and_Museum_%2828815276064%29.jpg/330px-9-11_Memorial_and_Museum_%2828815276064%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and Wall Street.
- Late afternoon: ride the Staten Island Ferry and visit Snug Harbor or walk the St. George waterfront, then ride back at golden hour.
- Evening: Broadway show.

**Day 3 (Wed): The Bronx**
- New York Botanical Garden ![New York Botanical Garden](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/The_New_York_Botanical_Garden_Visitor_Center.jpg/330px-The_New_York_Botanical_Garden_Visitor_Center.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail), which is at its best in mid-May (azaleas and lilacs). The Bronx Zoo is right next door if you'd rather do that.
- Lunch on Arthur Avenue, the Bronx's real Little Italy.
- Evening: a Yankees game ![Yankee Stadium](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/af/Yankee_Stadium_overhead_2010.jpg/330px-Yankee_Stadium_overhead_2010.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) if they're home. Check the 2028 schedule when it comes out.

**Day 4 (Thu): Queens → Brooklyn**
- Morning: take the 7 train to Flushing for the Unisphere ![Unisphere](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg/330px-Unisphere-2_%2827835155267%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) and a dumpling crawl.
- Midday: Long Island City, with skyline views from Gantry Plaza.
- Take the NYC Ferry down the East River to DUMBO, then explore Brooklyn Bridge Park.
- Sunset: walk the Brooklyn Bridge ![Brooklyn Bridge](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f0/Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg/330px-Brooklyn_Bridge_and_the_Lower_Manhattan_skyline_from_Pebble_Beach%2C_New_York.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) back into Manhattan.

**Day 5 (Fri): Central Park + the Met, then home**
- Walk Central Park ![Central Park](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Global_Citizen_Festival_Central_Park_New_York_City_from_NYonAir_%2815351915006%29.jpg/330px-Global_Citizen_Festival_Central_Park_New_York_City_from_NYonAir_%2815351915006%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail) (Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge).
- The Met ![Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/30/Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_%28The_Met%29_-_Central_Park%2C_NYC.jpg/330px-Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_%28The_Met%29_-_Central_Park%2C_NYC.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail): it's open late on Fridays, so a later train gives you more time.
- Afternoon or evening train back to DC.

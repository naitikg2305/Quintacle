### **Pull Workout Routine (3 Sets Each)**
**Goal:** Strength, hypertrophy, and posterior chain development  
**Order:** Heaviest/most demanding first (energy conservation)

---

#### **1. Deadlifts**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x6–8 (focus on form, control, and full range of motion)  
- **Variation:** Use a heavy weight (85–95% 1RM) or adjust based on fatigue.  
- **Tip:** Prioritize hip hinge mechanics and core engagement.  

---

#### **2. Weighted Pull-Ups**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x6–10 (use a weighted vest or dumbbells)  
- **Focus:** Latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, and biceps.  
- **Progression:** Increase weight gradually or use assisted pull-up machines.  

---

#### **3. Weighted Chin-Ups**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x6–8 (use a weighted belt or dumbbells)  
- **Focus:** Biceps, lats, and upper back.  
- **Tip:** Use a wide grip for lats or narrow for biceps.  

---

#### **4. Barbell Rows**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x8–10 (focus on eccentric control)  
- **Variation:** Use a barbell, dumbbells, or a weighted rope.  
- **Tip:** Keep your back flat and engage your lats by pulling the bar to your abdomen.  

---

#### **5. Bicep Curls**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x10–12 (use a moderate weight)  
- **Focus:** Biceps brachii (avoid overloading to prevent elbow strain).  
- **Tip:** Use a supinated grip (palms facing you) for maximum bicep activation.  

---

#### **6. Face Pulls (Cable or Band)**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x12–15 (focus on rear delts and upper back)  
- **Technique:** Pull the cable/elastic band to eye level, squeezing your shoulder blades.  

---

#### **7. Cable Pull-Aparts**  
- **Sets/Reps:** 3x12–15 (target rhomboids and middle trapezius)  
- **Tip:** Use a wide grip and pull the cable apart to engage the rear upper back.  

---

- **Deadlift Variations:** Romanian deadlifts or sumo deadlifts for added focus on hamstrings/glutes.

## A few tweaks to think about
- **Pull-ups and chin-ups back to back is a lot of vertical pulling.** If time or recovery gets tight, try alternating them by week: weighted pull-ups as the main lift one week, chin-ups the next. You could use the freed time for a **chest-supported row** or **single-arm cable row**, which gives you more back work without loading your lower back again.
- **Your curls only hit one angle.** Swapping one set for hammer curls also works the brachialis and forearms.
- **Progression:** do a double progression. Once you hit the top of the rep range on all 3 sets, add weight: about 2.5–5 kg on deadlifts and rows, 1.25–2.5 kg on pull-ups, chin-ups and curls.

# Quintacle — Intro / Vision

## The one-line pitch

A personal Jarvis. Not a chatbot — a **deterministic agent** built for one person's
specific life, the way Jarvis fully manages Tony Stark.

## The actual problem this solves

Naitik has ADHD. His brain runs constant parallel threads at once — people/relationships,
style and dressing, conflict/confrontation scenarios, fitness/gym, cars, and more —
all firing simultaneously, all day.

On top of that, he's constantly learning in the background: talking to AI, googling,
watching a video, figuring something out through trial and error — often literally
between sets at the gym. Right now, almost all of that knowledge **goes into the abyss**.
It's generated, used once, and lost. There's no system that captures it.

## The loop Quintacle is meant to close

```
learn something (AI chat / google / video / trial-and-error / real-world experience)
        -> summarize it into a note
        -> push
        -> lands in a specific, cleaned, organized personal database
```

The point isn't just note-taking — it's turning constant scattered
learning-in-motion into a structured, retrievable, growing personal knowledge base,
without breaking his flow to do it (e.g. while resting between gym sets).

## Life domains it's meant to actually manage

- Scheduling
- Financing
- Dressing / style
- Organizing his apartment
- Organizing his car
- Skills picked up incidentally and worth keeping — e.g. he learned how to help a
  friend buy a car; that kind of one-off learned skill should get captured too, not lost
- Social life / dating, treated as "just another algorithm" to learn like anything
  else — bar scenarios, pickup lines, casual dating, what he learns about women
  through actually being with them, how he handles friendships, and self-recognized
  patterns like being "perfectly avoidant." This is journal-style material, not
  facts to look up — the point is tracking his own patterns over time.

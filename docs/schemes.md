# Quintacle — Schemes (Node)

## What it is

A folder of literal life/business "schemes" — Plan A, Plan B, Plan C, Side Plan —
so the planning itself lives outside his head, freeing up mental space to actually
think about projects/business/everything else instead of holding it all in memory.

## Plans described so far

- **Base plan** — make 200k, save 80 grand in a year, buy a 450k townhouse.
- **Side hustle** — contract work; making money in crypto or cash.
- **Business plan** — multiple business ideas explored in parallel, each with its
  own viability assessment, rather than committing to one idea at a time.

## Why it's its own node

These are long-horizon, numbers-driven goals (income, savings, purchase targets)
that need to be checked against real progress — that's where `ledger.md` comes in
(actual money in/out validates whether the base plan's savings target is on track).
Schemes is the plan; ledger is the receipts.

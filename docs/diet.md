# Quintacle — Diet (Node)

## Current state

Eats the same food every day to reliably hit specific calorie targets.

## The improvement

Diversify the actual meals while keeping macros (and total kcal) exactly the same
as today — variety without moving the nutrition target. Then generate a grocery
list for the week that matches that diversified plan, and follow it exactly — so
cooking doesn't mean eating the same thing on repeat, but also doesn't mean
re-deciding what to eat/buy every single day.

## Why it's its own node

Same underlying principle as `outfits.md` — solve the decision once (this week's
meals + grocery list), then just execute, instead of re-deciding daily.

# Quintacle — Outfits (Node)

## The loop

Buy new clothes -> take a photo -> plan an outfit around it -> it goes into the
outfit folder. Same treatment for sunglasses, watches, matching colors.

## Why it's its own node

The point is to stop re-deciding what to wear from scratch every time. Once an
outfit (clothes + accessories + color matching) is planned once and stored, it's a
solved problem he can just pull up again — one more piece of daily mental load
handed off to the system instead of carried in his head.
